============================================================
  GURU Fundamental Scanner — Android Phone Setup Guide
============================================================

IMPORTANT — HOW THIS APP WORKS ON MOBILE
-----------------------------------------
GURU Fundamental Scanner is a web application that runs on
your Mac or Windows computer. Your phone connects to it over
your home/office WiFi network. The computer must be ON and
the app must be running for your phone to access it.

Think of it like a website running on your own computer —
your phone opens it in the browser, just like a website.

You have THREE options (choose the one that suits you):

  Option 1 : Open in Chrome browser (simplest, no install)
  Option 2 : Add to Home Screen (looks and feels like an app)
  Option 3 : Install as a proper APK file (most app-like)

All three are covered below.

============================================================
  PART 1 — FIND YOUR COMPUTER'S IP ADDRESS
============================================================

You need your computer's IP address so your phone can find it.

ON WINDOWS:
  1. Press  Win + R, type  cmd, press Enter
  2. Type:   ipconfig
  3. Look for "Wireless LAN adapter Wi-Fi:"
     Find the line:  IPv4 Address . . . . . : 192.168.X.X
  4. Write down that number (e.g. 192.168.1.5)

ON MAC:
  1. Open Terminal (Applications → Utilities → Terminal)
  2. Type:   ipconfig getifaddr en0
  3. The number shown is your IP (e.g. 192.168.1.5)

NOTE: Your computer and phone must be on the SAME WiFi network.


============================================================
  PART 2 — START THE APP ON YOUR COMPUTER
============================================================

ON WINDOWS:
  Double-click  run.bat  in the GURU_FUNDAMENTAL_SCANNER folder.

ON MAC:
  Open Terminal, go to the app folder, and run:  bash run.sh

Wait until you see:
  "Local URL: http://localhost:8501"
  "Network URL: http://192.168.X.X:8501"

The app is now ready.


============================================================
  OPTION 1 — OPEN IN CHROME BROWSER (Simplest)
============================================================

1. On your Android phone, open Google Chrome
2. In the address bar type:
      http://192.168.X.X:8501
   (replace 192.168.X.X with your computer's actual IP from Part 1)
3. The GURU Scanner login screen will appear
4. Log in with your phone number and OTP
5. Done! Use the app normally.

TIP: Bookmark this URL in Chrome so you can open it quickly next time.


============================================================
  OPTION 2 — ADD TO HOME SCREEN (Recommended — Free)
============================================================

This creates an icon on your phone's home screen.
When you tap it, it opens full screen like a real app.

STEP 1: Open the app in Chrome (follow Option 1 above first)

STEP 2: Add to Home Screen
  Method A — Chrome prompt (automatic):
    After opening the app, Chrome may show a banner at the bottom:
    "Add GURU Scanner to Home screen"
    Tap "Add" and you're done.

  Method B — Manual (if no banner appears):
    1. Tap the 3-dot menu (⋮) in the top-right corner of Chrome
    2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home screen"
       (or "Install app" if Chrome detects the PWA manifest)
    3. Tap "Add" in the popup
    4. The GURU Scanner icon appears on your home screen

STEP 3: Open from home screen
  Tap the GURU Scanner icon on your home screen.
  It will open in full-screen mode without the browser toolbar —
  it looks and feels like a native app.

IMPORTANT: The computer must still be running the app whenever
you open it from the home screen icon.


============================================================
  OPTION 3 — INSTALL AS APK FILE (Most App-Like)
============================================================

An APK is the Android app package format. This wraps the GURU
Scanner into a proper installable app. The app still connects
to your computer over WiFi — it is a WebView wrapper.

NOTE: You need to know your computer's static/fixed IP address
(or set a fixed IP in your WiFi router settings).

METHOD A — Using PWABuilder (Free, Microsoft's official tool)
--------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1: Keep the GURU Scanner running on your computer

STEP 2: On your computer, go to:
   https://www.pwabuilder.com

STEP 3: In the URL box, enter:
   http://192.168.X.X:8501
   (use your actual computer IP address)
   Click "Start"

STEP 4: PWABuilder analyses the app. When done, click:
   "Package for Stores"

STEP 5: Under "Android", click "Generate Package"
   - Select "Android" → "Download Package"
   - You will get a .apk file

STEP 6: Transfer the APK to your phone
   - Email it to yourself, or
   - Copy via USB cable to your phone's Downloads folder

STEP 7: Install the APK on your phone
   1. On your Android phone, go to:
      Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps
   2. Find "Files" or "My Files" app → Enable "Allow from this source"
   3. Open the APK file you transferred
   4. Tap "Install"
   5. Tap "Open" to launch

STEP 8: First launch
   The app opens and connects to your computer's Streamlit server.
   Log in as usual.

METHOD B — Using AppGyser (Easier, no account needed)
------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1: On your computer, go to:
   https://appgeyser.com

STEP 2: Click "Create App" → "Website"

STEP 3: Enter your app details:
   App Name : GURU Fundamental Scanner
   Website  : http://192.168.X.X:8501
   (replace with your computer's actual IP)

STEP 4: Follow the steps and download the generated APK

STEP 5: Install on your phone following Step 7 from Method A above.


============================================================
  TROUBLESHOOTING — ANDROID
============================================================

Problem : Chrome shows "This site can't be reached"
Fix     : Make sure the computer is running the app (run.bat/run.sh)
          and your phone is on the same WiFi. Check the IP address.

Problem : "Install unknown apps" option not visible
Fix     : Go to Settings → Biometrics and Security → Install Unknown Apps
          (location varies by Android version and phone brand)

Problem : APK says "App not installed" error
Fix     : Your phone may have blocked the install. Go to:
          Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps
          and allow installation for the app you used to open the APK.

Problem : App icon opens but shows "Unable to connect"
Fix     : Your computer's IP may have changed (dynamic IP).
          Run  ipconfig  again to find the new IP.
          Ask your router to assign a fixed/static IP to your computer.

Problem : Login OTP not received
Fix     : The OTP may appear directly on the login screen (grey box)
          if Twilio SMS is not configured. Check below the OTP input field.

Problem : App is slow on phone
Fix     : This is normal for a web app over WiFi. Try closing other
          browser tabs and apps on your phone.

============================================================

If you want the app accessible from anywhere without keeping
your computer on, you can deploy to the cloud:

  Option A : Streamlit Community Cloud (Free)
    - Push code to GitHub
    - Go to share.streamlit.io
    - Connect GitHub and deploy

  Option B : Railway.app or Render.com (Free tier available)
    - Similar GitHub-based deployment

  Option C : VPS (DigitalOcean, AWS, Azure)
    - Full control, runs 24/7

After cloud deployment, you get a public URL like:
   https://your-app.streamlit.app
Use that URL in your phone browser or in the APK builder above.
