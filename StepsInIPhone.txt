============================================================
  GURU Fundamental Scanner — iPhone Setup Guide
============================================================

IMPORTANT — HOW THIS APP WORKS ON iPHONE
------------------------------------------
GURU Fundamental Scanner is a web application that runs on
your Mac or Windows computer. Your iPhone connects to it over
your home/office WiFi network. The computer must be ON and
the app must be running for your iPhone to access it.

Think of it like a website running on your own computer —
your iPhone opens it in Safari, just like a website.

ABOUT iPHONE APP INSTALLATION:
Apple restricts installing apps outside the App Store.
Publishing to the App Store requires:
  - An Apple Developer account ($99 USD per year)
  - A Mac with Xcode
  - App review process (1-7 days)

The PRACTICAL and FREE alternative for personal use is:
  "Add to Home Screen" via Safari

This is covered in full detail below. When done, it looks and
feels exactly like an installed app on your iPhone.

You have TWO options:

  Option 1 : Open in Safari browser (simplest, no install)
  Option 2 : Add to Home Screen via Safari (looks like an app)

============================================================
  PART 1 — FIND YOUR COMPUTER'S IP ADDRESS
============================================================

You need your computer's IP address so your iPhone can find it.

ON WINDOWS:
  1. Press  Win + R, type  cmd, press Enter
  2. Type:   ipconfig
  3. Look for "Wireless LAN adapter Wi-Fi:"
     Find the line:  IPv4 Address . . . . . : 192.168.X.X
  4. Write down that number (e.g. 192.168.1.5)

ON MAC:
  1. Open Terminal (Applications → Utilities → Terminal)
  2. Type:   ipconfig getifaddr en0
  3. The number shown is your IP (e.g. 192.168.1.5)

NOTE: Your computer and iPhone must be on the SAME WiFi network.


============================================================
  PART 2 — START THE APP ON YOUR COMPUTER
============================================================

ON WINDOWS:
  Double-click  run.bat  in the GURU_FUNDAMENTAL_SCANNER folder.

ON MAC:
  Open Terminal, go to the app folder, and run:  bash run.sh

Wait until you see:
  "Local URL: http://localhost:8501"
  "Network URL: http://192.168.X.X:8501"

The app is now ready.


============================================================
  OPTION 1 — OPEN IN SAFARI BROWSER (Simplest)
============================================================

1. On your iPhone, open Safari
   (NOTE: Must be Safari — Chrome on iPhone cannot add to Home Screen)
2. In the address bar at the top, type:
      http://192.168.X.X:8501
   (replace 192.168.X.X with your computer's actual IP from Part 1)
3. Tap "Go" on the keyboard
4. The GURU Scanner login screen will appear
5. Log in with your phone number and OTP
6. Done! Use the app normally in Safari.

TIP: Bookmark this URL in Safari for quick access next time.
     Tap the Share button (box with arrow) → "Add Bookmark"


============================================================
  OPTION 2 — ADD TO HOME SCREEN VIA SAFARI (Recommended)
============================================================

This creates an app icon on your iPhone's home screen.
When you tap it, it opens full screen — no browser address bar —
exactly like a native app.

STEP 1: Open the app in Safari
  - Follow Option 1 above
  - Make sure the app is fully loaded (you can see the login screen)

STEP 2: Tap the Share button
  - Look for the Share icon at the bottom of Safari
    It looks like a box with an upward arrow: [  ↑  ]
  - Tap it
  - A menu slides up from the bottom

STEP 3: Find "Add to Home Screen"
  - Scroll left/right through the icons in the share menu
  - Tap "Add to Home Screen"
    (If you don't see it, scroll the bottom row of options to the right)

STEP 4: Name the app
  - A dialog appears with the name "GURU Scanner" (pre-filled)
  - You can change the name if you want
  - Tap "Add" in the top-right corner

STEP 5: Find the icon on your home screen
  - The GURU Scanner icon has been added to your home screen
  - Swipe to find it, or look on the last page of apps

STEP 6: Open from home screen
  - Tap the GURU Scanner icon
  - It opens in full-screen mode without the Safari browser bar
  - It looks and feels exactly like a native installed app

IMPORTANT: Every time you tap the home screen icon, your iPhone
connects to your computer over WiFi. The computer must be
running the app (run.bat or run.sh must be active).


============================================================
  ABOUT IPA FILES (iPhone App Package)
============================================================

An IPA is the iPhone equivalent of an Android APK file.
To create and install an IPA outside the App Store you need:

OPTION A — TestFlight (Apple's official beta testing tool)
  Requires:
  - Apple Developer account ($99 USD/year at developer.apple.com)
  - Mac computer with Xcode installed (free, 12GB download)
  - Build the app using Xcode
  - Upload to TestFlight via App Store Connect
  - Invite your own Apple ID as a tester
  Difficulty: Very high (requires iOS/macOS development knowledge)

OPTION B — AltStore (Sideloading without App Store)
  - Free, but requires re-signing the IPA every 7 days
  - Visit altstore.io for instructions
  - Requires a Mac or Windows PC to be connected occasionally
  Difficulty: Medium

OPTION C — Add to Home Screen (RECOMMENDED — No IPA needed)
  - Follow the steps in Option 2 above
  - Works exactly like an installed app
  - Free, instant, no developer account needed
  - This is the standard way web apps work on iPhone
  Difficulty: Very easy (5 minutes)

RECOMMENDATION: Use Option 2 (Add to Home Screen).
It is free, works instantly, and Apple officially supports
this feature. It is how all web apps are "installed" on iPhone.


============================================================
  TROUBLESHOOTING — iPHONE
============================================================

Problem : Safari shows "Safari Cannot Open the Page"
Fix     : Make sure the computer is running the app.
          Confirm your iPhone is on the same WiFi as your computer.
          Double-check the IP address you typed.

Problem : "Add to Home Screen" is not in the Share menu
Fix     : It may be hidden. In the share menu, scroll the second row
          of options to the right — it should appear there.
          Alternatively: tap "Edit Actions" at the bottom of the
          share menu and add "Add to Home Screen" to your favourites.

Problem : Home screen icon opens to blank white screen
Fix     : The app server on your computer has stopped.
          Run  run.bat  (Windows) or  bash run.sh  (Mac) again.
          Then tap the icon — it should work.

Problem : App works in Safari but home screen icon doesn't connect
Fix     : Your computer's IP address may have changed.
          Find the new IP (Part 1), open it in Safari, and
          add to home screen again (Option 2).

Problem : Login OTP not received via SMS
Fix     : OTP appears directly on the login screen (grey box)
          if Twilio SMS is not configured. Check below the OTP
          input field on the login page.

Problem : App looks too small / text is tiny on iPhone
Fix     : In Safari, pinch to zoom in. Or rotate phone to landscape.
          The app is designed for desktop — it works on mobile but
          desktop view gives the best experience.

Problem : Only Safari can add to Home Screen, not Chrome on iPhone
Fix     : This is correct — Apple only allows Safari for the
          "Add to Home Screen" feature on iPhone. Use Safari.
